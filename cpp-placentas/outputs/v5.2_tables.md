# Manuscript v5.2 tables: 9-covariate model as the primary analysis

Built by `scripts/72-v52-tables-reduced9-primary.R` from the pooled fits in script 71.

- **Sample:** 40,700 examined infants with 700 definite IH cases.
- **Imputations:** 50, pooled with Rubin's rules.
- **Adjusted models:** maternal age, race and ethnicity, education, annual family income, marital status, smoking, parity, infant sex and study site, with stabilized censoring weights re-estimated from both placental patterns and these 9 covariates.
- **Unadjusted models:** unweighted, with no covariates.
- **CIs:** 95% CIs, written as (lower, upper).
