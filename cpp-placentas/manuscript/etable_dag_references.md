## Gaps and cautions

- Arrow 17 (smoking -> MVM): no study shows that smoking causes MVM specifically. Smoking clearly raises abruption risk, and one 2016 study found no excess MVM in smokers. Consider keeping the arrow as an assumption or pointing it to abruption only.
- Arrow 18: Kovo 2017 supports chronic hypertension -> MVM, but Helfrich 2017 found no association for chronic hypertension alone. Cite both.
- Arrows 21 (race -> chorioamnionitis) and 28 (maternal smoking -> IH): assumption, no direct citation found.
- Arrow 9: no direct source was retrieved for maternal age -> pre-gestational (type 2) diabetes. Lean 2017 covers gestational diabetes only.
- Ding 2020: pooled estimates were not retrieved. Check the full text before naming specific factors from it.
- Rows marked partial, and DOIs noted as from memory, should be checked in PubMed before submission.
